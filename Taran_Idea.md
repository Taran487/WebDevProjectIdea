# Project idea 1

## campus Club management system
This website will allow users to manage clubs of a campus, events, announcements and user accounts as well. All these features will have CRUD implementation.
## Features
### user accounts
- Register
- Login/Logout
- View profile
- Edit profile
- Delete account
### club management
- Create a new club
- View all clubs
- Edit club information
- Delete a club
### Event Management
- Create events for clubs
- View upcoming events
- Update event details
- Delete events
### Announcements
- Post announcements for clubs
- View announcements
- Edit announcements
- Delete announcements
## DB Tables
- users
- clubs
- events
- announcements
## Website pages
- Home
- Login/Register
- Dashboard
- Clubs
- Club Details
- Events
- Announcements
- Profile

# Project idea 2

## Narration of the idea
Megha enjoys listening to English music and wants a website where she can organise her favourite albums, create playlists, and share her thoughts with other music lovers.

She visits the Music Album website let say BeatBox and creates a new account by entering her name, email address, and password.

After logging in, she sees a search bar on the home page.

She searches for Shakira.

The website sends a request to the music API, which returns a list of Shakira's albums along with their cover images, release dates, genres, and track lists.

Megha clicks on one of the albums to view more details.

The album details page displays:

Album cover
Artist name
Release year
Genre
Track list

After listening to the album,

Nextly, Megha creates a playlist called good vibes.

She adds albums and songs she enjoys, including tracks by shakira, justin bieber, and many other. Her playlist is saved to her account, and she can edit it anytime by adding or removing albums.

She also clicks the Favourite button on the album so she can quickly find it later. The website stores this in the favourites section of her account.

Later, she removes one of the albums from her good Vibes playlist because she wants to replace it with a newer release.
